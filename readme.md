# Ntlas · Smart QR Code Generator

A professional, modern, and interactive QR Code generator built with a sleek glassmorphism UI.  
Create QR codes for **Text**, **Wi-Fi networks**, and **vCards (Contacts)** instantly.

**100% Client-Side.** No servers. No data tracking. Your data remains private on your device.

---

### ✨ Why use this?
- Generate professional QR codes without installing any software.
- Create smart QR codes for Wi-Fi access or digital business cards (vCards).
- Customize the look with neon frames and custom colors.

---

### 🔧 Key Features
- **🔗 Text/Link Generation:** Convert any URL or text into a scannable QR code.
- **📶 Wi-Fi QR Codes:** Generate a QR code that lets users connect to your Wi-Fi instantly (WPA/WPA2, WEP, or Open).
- **📇 vCard (Contact) QR Codes:** Create a digital business card that saves directly to the user's phone contacts.
- **🎨 Visual Customization:** Choose between **Square** or **Round** neon frames, and customize the neon and QR colors.
- **🖼️ Logo Embedding:** Upload a custom logo to be placed in the center of the QR code.
- **📱 Export Options:** Download the QR code as a PNG image, copy it to your clipboard, or share it natively on mobile.
- **🌍 Bilingual Interface:** Full support for Arabic (AR) and French (FR).
- **🎬 Immersive Background:** Video background with a cinematic dark overlay.

---

### 🚀 Quick Start
1. Select the tab: **"Text/Link"**, **"Wi-Fi"**, or **"Contact"**.
2. Enter your data (URL, Wi-Fi credentials, or contact info).
3. Customize the frame, colors, and logo (optional).
4. Click **"Generate QR"**.
5. Download, copy, or share your result instantly.
